Cambodian Parliament Condemns Establishment of Sam Rainsy-Led Overseas Assembly

Cambodia’s National Assembly has condemned the establishment of an overseas Cambodian people’s assembly led by Sam Rainsy, describing it as illegal and a violation of the Cambodian Constitution.
In a statement dated 27 October 2026, the National Assembly said the body, established in Paris, France, on 24 October, did not represent the will of the Cambodian people and was inconsistent with democratic principles.
The assembly said Cambodians had elected members of the seventh legislature in the national election on 23 July 2023. It accused the overseas political group of seeking to create instability and overthrow the Cambodian government without respecting democratic processes.
The National Assembly also urged Cambodians at home and abroad to remain vigilant against activities it said could undermine national solidarity and unity.
Seun Sam, a policy analyst at the Royal Academy of Cambodia, said establishing a political institution abroad was unnecessary and could further divide the voices of the Cambodian people. He warned that such activities could give other countries an opportunity to interfere in Cambodia’s internal affairs and portray the country as divided on the international stage.
He urged Cambodians living overseas to contribute to the country’s development rather than establish political institutions opposed to the government.
Seun Sam said that although people might hold different views on government policies, they should seek ways to contribute to national development without creating institutions that could deepen divisions.
“I think Cambodians living abroad should speak with one voice alongside the Cambodian government and avoid doing anything against their own country,” he said. “Even if they disagree over certain policies, establishing a government of this kind serves no purpose.”
He added that Cambodia should focus on building a stronger country, while Cambodians abroad could still contribute to national development in many ways.
In contrast, a member of the 23 October Independent Cambodian Government said the newly established overseas assembly represented the genuine will of the Cambodian people.
On 24 October, members of the Cambodian community in France and Cambodian political groups living abroad gathered at the Poly d’Or restaurant on the outskirts of Paris to inaugurate the body, known as the “Overseas Cambodian People’s Assembly”.
A total of 47 members were elected to the overseas assembly. They are Cambodian political figures living in France, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea and other European countries. The assembly also voted to express confidence in the 23 October Independent Cambodian Government, led by Sam Rainsy.
