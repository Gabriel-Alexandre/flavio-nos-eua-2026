Donald Trump addressed Victor Orbán’s election defeat for the first time two days later, expressing both recognition and distance in his statement. Responding to a question from the Italian
Corriere della Sera
, the U.S. President spoke about his former ally, whom he called a good leader, but referred to their relationship in the past tense.
When asked if he regretted Vikor Orbán’s defeat, Donald Trump said the Hungarian Prime Minister “was my friend.” The president added that he still considers him a good person.
The U.S. President specifically highlighted Orbán’s immigration policy, which he assessed as particularly successful.
He did a good job handling immigration; he did not let people come in and ruin his country, as happened in Italy,”
he noted.
The president also shared his opinion on Péter Magyar, according to an
X
post by Janathan Karl,
ABC News
’s Washington correspondent. “I think the new man’s going to do a good job — he’s a good man,” Trump is quoted as saying. As the Washington correspondent wrote, Trump stated that
he did not know if it would have made a difference if he had gone to Hungary on April 7 instead of Vice President JD Vance.
President Trump reportedly also emphasized that the new prime minister-elect was previously part of Fidesz’s inner circle and, as such, holds views on immigration similar to those of the outgoing prime minister. “I think he’s going to be good,” he summarized his opinion of Magyar.
