U.S. Vice President JD Vance commented on Fidesz’s defeat. According to the second-most powerful man in the United States, Viktor Orbán is a transformative leader who has significantly shaped the country’s development for 16 years. In an interview with
Fox News
, he emphasized that the now former Hungarian Prime Minister is a wonderful person who has done a very good job.
“One of the reasons we supported him in the elections was not that we did not understand the polls. We knew, of course, that Viktor Orbán would likely lose. But we decided to act because he was one of the few European politicians willing to stand up to the Brussels bureaucracy that has harmed the United States.
When European officials attacked American companies, the ex-PM was often the only one who stood up for and defended American interests,”
JD Vance emphasized. The U.S. Vice President noted that his trip to Hungary was not based on the expectation of an easy victory for Viktor Orbán. In his view, the goal had been to stand by a person who has long supported the United States.
He added that this matter was not about Russia or Europe in general. It was about the interests of the United States and the fact that the ex-Hungarian Prime Minister was a reliable partner both for himself and for President Donald Trump, and for the United States in general.
“It is a shame he lost. I am sure we can work well with the new Hungarian Prime Minister, Péter Magyar.
But this path was not a mistake, because it is important to support the people, even if we do not win every battle,”
Vance added.
Trump had repeatedly and publicly called on Hungarians before the Hungarian elections to vote for Orbán’s reelection. He described the prime minister as a strong and influential leader and praised him for helping reach new heights of cooperation between the United States and Hungary.
