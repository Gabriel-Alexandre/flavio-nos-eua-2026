The
61st Munich Security Conference
(
MSC 2025
) was the annual meeting of the
Munich Security Conference
, that took place from 14 February to 16 February 2025 in
Munich
, Germany. A few days earlier the Munich Security Report had been published. Its title served as thematic framework for the conference: 'multipolarization' - A state of global reorganization between promising opportunities and endangered communality, especially in the management of crises and threats. According to the report, “depolarization” and substantial reforms of the international order are now needed more than ever.
[
1
]
During the conference, a series of US statements at the conference sparked controversy and unrest among the attending European politicians.
[
2
]
Key outcomes of the conference include further weakening of the transatlantic alliance,
[
2
]
[
3
]
US attitude shift about the
war in Ukraine
–
and Europe in general, increased US pressure on Europe to further raise
defence spending
, calls by the US for Europe to further increase military aid to Ukraine, and discord between US and Europe on topics as diverse as election interference, trade, freedom of speech and democratic values.

]

The conference was opened on 14 February 2025 by German president

During the event, Ukrainian president

JD Vance

Vance also claimed that European states were failing to uphold free speech and democratic values, criticizing the

and the decision to cancel the results of the
2024 Romanian presidential election
because of

in the campaign.
