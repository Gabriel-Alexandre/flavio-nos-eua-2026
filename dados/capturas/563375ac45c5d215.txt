Marco Rubio Postpones Meeting with Foreign Minister Anita Orbán
Hungary Today
2026.10.27.
Print without picture
Print with picture
The meeting between Hungarian Foreign Minister Anita Orbán and U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has been removed from the U.S. State Department’s official schedule for Tuesday,
Magyar Nemzet
reported.
It appears that Marco Rubio’s schedule for Tuesday has changed: the latest daily schedule from the U.S. Department of State, released Monday evening, no longer includes the planned meeting with Hungarian Foreign Minister Anita Orbán. The previous weekly schedule clearly indicated that Marco Rubio would receive his Hungarian counterpart at the State Department building on Tuesday.
The change is noteworthy because Anita Orbán herself had previously announced that she would hold talks with Marco Rubio in Washington on October 27.
The meeting was subsequently confirmed by the U.S. State Department’s preliminary schedule.
However, the latest daily schedule released Monday evening paints a different picture of the diplomatic agenda. According to the document, Marco Rubio will accompany U.S. President Donald Trump to Texas on Tuesday. At the same time, the U.S. Secretary of State’s schedule reveals that diplomatic work at the State Department will continue on Tuesday as well. At 2:00 p.m., Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau will meet with Tongan Prime Minister Lord Fakafanua at the State Department building.
In response to an
inquiry
from
Magyar Nemzet
, the press secretary of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs stated that Foreign Minister Anita Orbán’s meeting with the U.S. Secretary of State, originally scheduled for Tuesday, has been postponed to the second half of the week at the request of the U.S. side. The Hungarian foreign minister is already in Washington and is waiting for Marco Rubio to return to the U.S. capital.
